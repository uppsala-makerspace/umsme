## 1. What you are buying

You are buying the item or service shown on the purchase page, from Uppsala
Makerspace. The price is shown before you pay. For items where you set the amount
yourself — clay you weigh, or a donation — the amount you enter is what is
charged.

## 2. Payment

- All payments are exclusive of VAT, as the association is VAT-exempt.
- Payment is made with Swish. The purchase is complete once Swish has confirmed
  the payment.
- A receipt is sent to your registered email address.

## 3. Right of withdrawal

For physical goods you have, as a consumer, 14 days to withdraw from the purchase
from the day you received the item. The right does not apply if the item has been
used or cannot be returned in essentially unchanged condition — this covers clay
and other consumables.

For courses and other services the right of withdrawal ends once the service has
been performed. If you cannot attend a course, please contact us as early as
possible.

## 4. If something is wrong

Contact us if an item is faulty or a course is cancelled. A refund is then made to
the account or Swish number we agree on.

## 5. Handling of your details

The purchase is linked to your member account so that you can see it under My
purchases. Any comment you leave — for example the name of the person attending a
course — is stored with the purchase.
