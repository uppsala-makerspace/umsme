# Expenses

This guide shows how to buy things for the makerspace and get your money back, how to approve other members' expenses if you are an approver, and how a group keeps track of its budget.

## 1. Help with the shopping

The makerspace is run by its members, and much of what it needs is bought by members themselves: consumables for a workshop, a spare part for a machine, coffee for a work day. The association is glad when you help out when you see that something is needed. You pay, upload the receipt in the app, and get the money back.

What may be bought on an expense account is decided by the groups that have access to it. They have been given a budget and decide for themselves how it is used. The account often has a description reminding you what it is meant for. It shows below the account when you choose it in the form, and in the list under **Expenses** → **Accounts**. If you are unsure, ask the group before you buy.

## 2. How it works

Expenses are made on an **expense account**. An expense account can be shared by several groups. The wood workshop's account, for example, can be used by both the wood workshop's steering group and its materials team. You can make expenses on the accounts of the groups you are an active member of. If you are not in any such group, the **Expenses** button on the home page is greyed out and you can't make expenses.

An expense is one of two kinds:

- **Receipt** — you have already paid and want the money back. The treasurer transfers the amount to the bank account in your profile.
- **Invoice** — the association should pay an invoice directly to the supplier. The treasurer pays the invoice, and you get no money yourself.

You upload the receipt or invoice as a photo or a PDF.

Every expense must be **approved** by someone other than you, one of the account's approvers. Nobody can approve their own expense. Once approved, the expense shows as **Confirmed**, and the treasurer can pay it.

An expense goes through these steps:

**Pending** → **Submitted** → **Confirmed** → **Reimbursed** (receipt) or **Paid** (invoice)

If the approver finds something wrong, the expense can be **Rejected**. You then see why, can correct it and submit it again.

## 3. Make an expense

### Add your bank account first

The treasurer needs to know where to send the money. Open the **☰** menu and choose **My Profile**. Under **Bank account**, fill in bank, clearing number and account number exactly as your bank shows them. If the account isn't your own, also fill in the account holder's name.

The Bank account section only shows for members who may make expenses.

![My Profile with the Bank account section filled in](../screens/expenses-profile-bank-en.png)

The bank account is only needed for receipts. An invoice is paid to the supplier.

### Create a new expense

Every expense belongs to an expense account. You can start in two ways:

- **From the account's page.** Each expense account has a page of its own, which you reach from the group's page. Tapping **New expense** there picks the account for you. This is easiest if you have access to several accounts.
- **From Expenses.** Tap **Expenses** on the home page and then **New expense**. You then choose the account in the form in the next step.

The app then asks whether you have already paid:

- Choose **Receipt** if you have paid and want the money back.
- Choose **Invoice** if the association should pay.

![New expense with the Receipt and Invoice choices](../screens/expenses-new-choose-en.png)

Then photograph the receipt or invoice, or upload it as an image or PDF. The expense is saved as a draft right away, so you can photograph the receipt in the shop and fill in the rest later.

![The step to photograph or upload the receipt](../screens/expenses-new-capture-en.png)

### Fill in the details

Fill in:

- **Amount (kr)**.
- **Expense account** — which account the expense belongs to. The field is empty unless you started from the account's page.
- **Place of purchase** — where you bought it. For an invoice the field is called **Supplier**.
- **Receipt date** — or **Invoice date** for an invoice.
- **Due date** — invoices only, and required.
- **Note** — what you bought and what it is for.

![The form filled in for a receipt](../screens/expenses-form-receipt-en.png)

> **Get a separate receipt for what should be reimbursed.** If you shop both for the association and for yourself, ask for two receipts at the till. If you still end up with a mixed receipt, the note must say clearly which items should be reimbursed and what they cost. Report a receipt on one expense account only, even if the items belong to several. Then choose the account that covers most of the items.

You can switch the type at the top of the form as long as the expense hasn't been submitted.

### Submit

Tap **Submit**. The expense becomes **Submitted** and is visible to the account's approvers. To continue later, tap **Save** instead.

### Follow your expense

Under **My expenses** you see all your expenses, ordered by status. Each row shows the amount, the account and the date of the latest step. Invoices are marked **Invoice** with their due date until they are paid.

![My expenses with expenses in different statuses](../screens/expenses-mine-en.png)

### If you need to change something

A submitted expense can't be edited. Open it and tap **Retract** to turn it back into a draft, which you can correct and submit again.

If the expense has been **Rejected**, the reason is shown at the top. Correct what is needed and submit again.

![A rejected expense with the reason at the top](../screens/expenses-rejected-en.png)

Once the expense is confirmed you can no longer change it. Contact the treasurer if something is wrong.

## 4. Approve an expense

This section is for you if you are an **approver** for an expense account. Approvers are chosen among the group's members, and each account should have at least two.

As an approver you get an extra tab, **Approve**, under Expenses. A number on the tab shows how many expenses are waiting.

![The Approve tab with waiting expenses](../screens/expenses-approve-list-en.png)

Open an expense and check that:

- the receipt or invoice is legible and matches the amount,
- the expense is on the right account,
- the purchase is reasonable for the group.

![An expense to review, with the Approve and Reject buttons](../screens/expenses-approve-detail-en.png)

Then tap **Approve** or **Reject**:

- **Approve** makes the expense **Confirmed**, and it moves on to the treasurer.
- **Reject** requires a reason. It is shown to whoever submitted the expense, so say what needs correcting.

You can't approve your own expenses; someone else approves them. Expenses you have reviewed show under **Recently reviewed** on the same tab.

## 5. Follow the group's budget

An expense account can have a budget for the year. The board sets it.

### All accounts

Under **Expenses** → **Accounts**, tap **Budget** at the top right. At the top you see a summary for all accounts you have access to: how much is spent, how much is left, and the whole budget. Below it each account has a card with the same figures. A bar that turns red means the account is over budget.

![The budget view with the summary and the account cards](../screens/expenses-budget-en.png)

**Count as spent** controls which expenses count: submitted, confirmed and reimbursed/paid. By default confirmed and reimbursed/paid count.

**Booked at reimbursement** shows where the costs ended up in the association's books. When the treasurer marks an expense as reimbursed or paid, the treasurer also chooses a **bookkeeping account**, for example 6110 Office supplies. The bookkeeping account is not the same as the expense account:

- The **expense account** decides which group the expense belongs to, and so who may make and approve it.
- The **bookkeeping account** is a bookkeeping detail chosen by the treasurer, saying what kind of cost it is.

Expenses on the same expense account can therefore be booked on different bookkeeping accounts. The summary at the top shows the split for all accounts. For a single account, tap the arrow in the card's top right corner. Only reimbursed and paid expenses have a bookkeeping account, so the others don't show here.

### One account

Tapping an account takes you to the account's page. It shows the year's budget at the top, and below it every expense on the account, including other members'. Choose the year and which statuses to show with the pickers at the top.

![An expense account's page with the budget box and the expenses](../screens/expenses-account-en.png)

From here you can also start a new expense on that account.
