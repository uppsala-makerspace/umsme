# How the app supports the association

This guide shows where you find the association's parts in the app and what you can do there: groups, workshops and areas of interest, certificates and tests, tutorials, activities and events, expenses, and messages and notifications.

The background — what the groups are for and how the association is organised — is on the page [How the association works](https://www.uppsalamakerspace.se/en/how-the-association-works/) on the website. If you are a new member, start with [New members — getting started](newMembers.html).

## 1. Finding your way around

The app has three ways to get around:

- **The bar at the bottom** — **Home**, **Doors**, **Workshops**, **Map** and **Calendar**.
- **The menu** (☰ at the top left) — among others **Messages**, **Certificates**, **Groups**, **Tutorials** and, if you are in a group with an expense account, **Expenses**.
- **The home page** — cards for **Activities**, **Groups**, **Tutorials** and **Expenses**, and a box that shows if you have new messages.

## 2. Groups

Tap **Groups** on the home page or in the menu. The list shows all groups, the ones you are in first. You can search and filter by group type: **Steering group**, **Responsibility group** and **Function group**, or **Member / group responsible** for your own.

A group's page shows its description, the group responsible, the group's rules and Slack channel, and which workshop it belongs to. From here you get in:

- **Join group** — in an open group you are in straight away.
- **Request to join** — in other groups. It then says *Your request is awaiting approval.* until someone has approved it. If you change your mind, tap **Withdraw request**.
- If it says the group does not take join requests, talk to the group when you meet. They add you with your membership number, which you find under **My Account**.

To stop, tap **Leave group**.

The member list is only shown to the group's own members. If you are in the group you also see the group's **Expense accounts**, and whoever may approve new members sees **Pending requests** and can **Add member** by membership number.

## 3. Workshops and areas of interest

Tap **Workshops** in the bottom bar. At the top you switch between **Workshops** and **Areas of interest**.

A workshop's page shows:

- the description, and the status if the workshop is not established (**On trial**, **Forming**, **Decommissioned**),
- cards for **Rules**, the Slack channel, **Search tools** and **Tutorials**,
- a **Calendar** of upcoming events,
- **Workshop certificates**,
- **Get involved in the workshop** — the steering group and its responsibility groups, with how many are in each. Tap a group to join or request to join.

## 4. Certificates

Tap **Certificates** in the menu. There you find **My Certificates**, **Available Certificates** and **Locked Certificates** — the ones you cannot take yet because a prerequisite is missing.

**Asking for a certificate.** When a certifier is present and ready, open the certificate and tap **Ask for confirmation**. The request is valid for two hours. The certifier confirms it in their app.

- If the certifier leaves a comment instead of confirming, you see it under **Feedback from certifier**. Practise some more, and tap **Re-request** next time you meet.
- Once the certificate is confirmed, you see who certified you, when, and how long it is valid.

**If you are a certifier** you also have the **Requests** tab, with the requests waiting for you. Open a request, write a **Public comment** (the applicant sees it) or a **Private comment** (only certifiers and administrators see it), and tap **Confirm** or **Deny**. Denying needs a public comment, so the applicant knows what is missing.

## 5. Tests

If a certificate says *This certificate is issued via a knowledge test.*, you get it through a test. Tap **Start test**. You answer one question at a time and can go back and forth. If you stop, you can later carry on where you were with **Continue test**.

When you hand in, you see at once whether you **Passed!** or **Failed**, and how many you got right. The certificate shows how many attempts you have left. If you have used them all, contact an administrator.

## 6. Tutorials

The **Tutorials** card on the home page and **Tutorials** in the menu lead to the association's tutorial site, where you are reading this guide. Workshops and function groups can also have their own tutorials; then the **Tutorials** card is on their page.

## 7. Activities and events

**Calendar** in the bottom bar shows the events. Choose **Upcoming**, **Past** or **My registrations**, and tap **Load more events** to see more. The **Activities** card on the home page shows the activities, and you can filter them by workshop.

An event's page shows the time, place, hosts and what is required. That is where you do most things:

- **Sign up**, or **Join the queue** if it is full. When a seat becomes free you are told, and then you tap **Take the seat** — first come, first served.
- **Check in** when check-in is open, if the event requires it.
- **Withdraw** or **Leave the queue** if you cannot come.
- **Add to calendar** — saves the event in your phone's calendar.

If you cannot sign up, the page says why, for example that a certificate is required.

On an activity's page you can tap **I am interested** to be told when a new event is put up, and **No longer** when you no longer want that.

**If you are in a group that organises activities** you can create them with **New activity** and put up events with **New event**. As a host you see the participants on the event's page, mark who is **Present**, add someone who came without signing up, and tap **Close attendance** afterwards. If the event gives a certificate and you are a certifier, tap **Certify or comment** next to the participant.

## 8. Expenses

If you are in a group with an expense account, you find **Expenses** on the home page and in the menu. There are the tabs **My expenses**, **Approve** (if you approve others' expenses) and **Accounts**. How to add a receipt or an invoice, approve others' expenses and follow a budget is in the guide [Expenses](../expenses/expenses.html).

## 9. Messages and notifications

**Messages** in the menu has two tabs:

- **Announcements** — what goes to all members, tagged **Newsletter** or **Information**.
- **Personal messages** — what concerns only you, tagged **Personal**: the welcome message, payment confirmations, reminders and news about events.

New messages have a green dot. The home page also shows how many announcements and personal messages you have.

**Notifications** are messages that pop up on your phone. The bell at the top of the app shows the notifications that have come to this phone, with a link to the whole message.

Under **Settings** → **Notification Settings** you allow notifications with **Allow notifications** and choose which kinds you want: **Membership reminders**, **Account & payment messages**, **Announcements** and **Activities and events**. The choices only apply to the notifications — you still get the emails.

On an iPhone, notifications only work in the installed app. See [Install the app](installApp.html) and [Allow location and notifications](permissions.html).
